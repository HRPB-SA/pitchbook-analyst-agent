### 1.1 Grammar and Punctuation

> 🔴 **ERROR** | ["have beat"](<citation:heading:Only three verticals have beat the public benchmark>)
> "Only three verticals have beat the public benchmark over the past three years" (chart title, p. 9)
> **Fix:** "have beaten" — past participle required after "have."

> 🔴 **ERROR** | ["control still rests"](<citation:heading:in OpenAI’s governance case, control still rests>)
> "The companies closest to the technology have to rely on rented computing power, have the thinnest margins after paying for the models, and, in OpenAI's governance case, control still rests with its nonprofit foundation even after the October 2025 conversion." (p. 11)
> **Fix:** Faulty parallelism — the third item abandons the subject ("The companies … have …, have …, and control still rests"). Rewrite, e.g.: "…and, in OpenAI's case, still answer to a nonprofit foundation even after the October 2025 conversion." Also check "after paying for the models" — these ARE the model builders; "after paying for compute" appears to be the intended economics.

> 🟡 **WARNING** | ["Q3 printing"](<citation:heading:the Q3 printing will give back>)

> **Fix:** "the Q3 print" (market idiom); "printing" reads as a typo. Prefer "We forecast" over "We are forecasting."

### 1.3 Clarity and Awkward Phrasing

> 🔵 **STYLE** | ["calibrate to it"](<citation:heading:more rapidly than its underwriters could calibrate to it>)
> "…serving as evidence that the IPO window has opened more rapidly than its underwriters could calibrate to it." (p. 19)
> **Suggestion:** "…opened faster than its underwriters could calibrate to." The trailing "it" doubles the object and the "serving as evidence" participle floats.

> 🔵 **STYLE** | ["level of growth it shows"](<citation:heading:rather thin in comparison with the level of growth it shows>)
> "A secondary market for unicorn shares has recently developed but is rather thin in comparison with the level of growth it shows." (p. 16)
> **Suggestion:** Thin compared with its own growth is a muddled comparison — say what is meant: "…has grown quickly but remains thin relative to the primary market" (or to the universe it prices).

> 🔵 **STYLE** | ["While the impact"](<citation:heading:While the impact SpaceX is having on the entire VC ecosystem>)
> "While the impact SpaceX is having on the entire VC ecosystem is unquestionable, and removing it from the totals reveals figures a fraction of the size, we believe a real recovery is underway…" (p. 18)
> **Suggestion:** The "and" welds two unlike clauses inside the concession. Split: "SpaceX's impact on the ecosystem is unquestionable, and stripping it out shrinks the totals to a fraction of the headline. We still believe a real recovery is underway…"

> 🔵 **STYLE** | ["points to a rise"](<citation:heading:hot pricing points to a rise in the number of listings>)
> Key takeaway: "Q2's hot pricing points to a rise in the number of listings in the back half of the year." (p. 3)
> **Suggestion:** The body hedges this same claim twice ("this suggestion is made precisely at the point when the trend changed"; "The direction remains the same, but the size of the effect does not," p. 20). Soften the takeaway to match the body's own caveat, e.g. "…suggests a rise in listings."
