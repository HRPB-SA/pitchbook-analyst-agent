## Basis discipline (read first, applies to every number)

Three incompatible data bases are in play. Never put a figure from one next to a
figure from another without labeling it.

- **Monitor basis** (Unicorn Monitor Data Q2 2026): the universe, vertical, index-
  return, and top-20 three-mark basis. Headline: **1,556 active / $8,496.57B**
  aggregate. Curated AI index **430 / $4,143.69B**.
- **Unicorn 20 basis** (Morningstar PitchBook Unicorn 20): daily model marks for
  20 marquee names. 20 total **$5,019.04B** at 6/30/2026.
- **Primary Tracker basis** (Final Q2 2026 Quarterly Unicorn Tracker, deal-level):
  the deal-dynamics and fallen-list basis. Headline: **1,743 active / $8,232.51B**.
  Broad AI tag **865 / $6,023.32B**.

Standing rule (from PT-Crosswalk): universe / valuation / curated-vertical / top-20
performance -> Monitor (and Unicorn 20 for the 20 marks). Deal dynamics
(concentration, RVVC, step-ups, down rounds, cadence, syndicates, investors),
exits, dormancy, the fallen list, and the country cut -> Primary Tracker. Dormancy
is the one metric where the two bases agree (47.6%). NEVER blend AI 430 with AI
865, or SaaS 457 with SaaS 968.

Key cross-basis gaps to disclose, not average:
- New unicorns in Q2 2026: Monitor **24** vs Primary Tracker **84** (definitional:
  qualifying event vs deal-level minting).
- Aggregate: Monitor $8,496.6B vs PT $8,232.5B (constituent-inclusion rules).
- 2021 formation: Monitor 618 vs PT 634 (both label 2021 the peak year).

## The through-line

The $8.5T headline is real but concentrated (top-10 47.8% of value, top-5 62.4% of
