# Previous reports

Drop past reports here (.docx anywhere in this tree, subfolders fine). The
style profiler reads them all: `python3 -m engine style` measures sentence
geometry, evidence density, citation patterns, and section cadence into
style/style_profile.json, which every new report is composed against. The
more of your library lives here, the tighter the voice match.

`databricks-brick-by-brick-jul2026/` is the archived July 2026 hand-built
initiation (build scripts, charts, outputs, validation log) that the engine
was generalized from.
