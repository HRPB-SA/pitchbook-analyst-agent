## (d) Where the thesis got weaker, and where it got stronger

Thesis tested: frontier AI cost architecture is structurally incompatible with investable return profiles. Verdict of the refresh: the thesis survives as a statement about commitments and margins, not about profits. It is weaker at the operating-result level and stronger at the commitment level, and the next mispricing is specific, not general.

**Weaker**
1. Anthropic printed a positive adjusted operating income in Q2-2026 on $11.5-11.6B of quarterly revenue (Bloomberg Aug 14, T2; projected $559M per Reuters, ~5.1% adjusted margin; non-GAAP, excludes stock-based compensation per relays, definition unpublished). One lab, one quarter, one non-GAAP measure: real counter-evidence, and less than "first operating profit" implied.
2. Revenue beat every plan in the ledger at both labs. Anthropic's ladder is now T2 at every step ($787M Q2-25 → $4.73B Q1-26 → >$11.5B Q2-26; run-rate $9B → $30B → $47B → $65B); OpenAI's run-rate doubled from >$20B to >$40B in seven months. Multiples compressed from 34.1x/34.1x (July basis) to 24.6x/21.3x with no new marks.
3. Vendor-backstopped financing and unit-cost deflation lower the cost of capacity: TPU lease SPV at Treasuries +1pt / 5.75% / 8.5% with Broadcom and Google support; Nvidia's $105B residual-value guaranty on OpenAI's Ohio leases; Nvidia holding the Lambda lease; powered-shell rent at $1.76-2.4M per MW-year; serving stacks near $0.22 per million output tokens on efficient MoE models (benchmark, not frontier COGS).

**Stronger**
1. OpenAI's H1-2026 operating loss was $21.6B on $12.4B of revenue (1.74x, widening from $9.3B to $12.3B a quarter), with the compute plan revised up twice in 2026 ($600B → $750B through 2030) and the cash-burn forecast raised by ~$111B.
2. The next mispricing, not yet consensus: on Anthropic's own 2028 plan ($190-200B revenue, 77% gross margin, $22B training) the annual compute envelope is ~$66-68B. Priced contracts already take ~$53B in 2028, and the Google Cloud commitment alone, reported at ~$200B over five years (T3, unverified; Alphabet, Google and Broadcom filings are silent), would add ~$40B a year. The 77% margin, the $22B training budget and the take-or-pay status of the gigawatt deals cannot all be true. The public S-1 commitments note will say which one gives.
3. Margin plans were missed at both labs while revenue plans were beaten (OpenAI 33% vs 46% plan; Anthropic lowered its projection), and the July finding that both names trade at an identical 34.1x is dead: the relative multiple flips sign inside the disputed gross-to-net haircut range (Anthropic at a 15% premium at 39.75%, a 5% discount at 27%), so ~$190B of relative value rides on an accounting basis the S-1 has not yet disclosed.
