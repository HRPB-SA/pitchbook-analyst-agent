## What they do

Builds the robot's 'motor nerves' rather than its brain: a general-purpose robot control platform ('Robot Academy') built on AI Self-Exploration, letting a robot independently discover its own joint structure, sensor configuration, range of motion and physical constraints, then generate hardware-specific control. Aims to remove the need to redesign control systems and rebuild training data whenever hardware changes.
