## Open questions

MATERIAL, and the central thesis-fit question: the 'zero pharmacist involvement' claim sits in real tension with pharmacy-board regulation. Ohio Rule 4729:5-3-17 requires pharmacist verification during an initial period plus ongoing sampling, and appears to require pharmacist ID capture prior to dispensation even under reduced oversight. No public source confirms which states, if any, have approved a genuinely zero-pharmacist model for Queue specifically; incumbents (ScriptPro, Parata, RxSafe) operate via remote pharmacist verification, not full autonomy. Second ask: the actual pricing model with the named pharmacy-chain pilot.
