## What they do

Robot foundation-model infrastructure out of Copenhagen and Stockholm (Qualia ApS / Qualia Studios): GPU-backed environments plus APIs for training, fine-tuning and shipping vision-language-action models onto robot controllers. Robot-agnostic and model-agnostic; sub-50ms on-robot policy execution with canary rollout and no operator in the loop; software-primary with no hardware ambition. Forward-deployed into live European factory and logistics lines (Munich, Lyon, Lisbon) with 3-6 months kickoff-to-canary and a weekly retraining cadence.
