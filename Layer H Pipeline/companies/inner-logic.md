### Decision reasoning

Durability 6/10 BORDERLINE. Clears the copilot screen cleanly - no assist framing anywhere, and the founders demonstrated genuine machine autonomy at Johns Hopkins (autonomous gallbladder-removal steps in live animals). But it FAILS THE STRICT END-TO-END TEST ONE LEVEL UP: this is infrastructure-for-autonomy sold to device manufacturers, so the autonomy belongs to the customer's future device, not to Inner Logic's own product. System of Record (2) and Workflow Depth (2) carry it - regulatory-grade traceable evidence inside an FDA design-control workflow is genuinely the layer under the device. Outcome Pricing is 0: no pricing model disclosed anywhere. Two validator flags resolved by PitchBook: Valia Ventures IS a real investor (the company's own 8-logo wall is incomplete, the press release was right), and the 'Backed since 2025' conflict on GC's portfolio page does NOT imply hidden earlier equity - raised-to-date is exactly $11.5M and the only prior event was a non-equity Johns Hopkins accelerator placement.
