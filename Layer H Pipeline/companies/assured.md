## What they do

An NCQA-adjacent Credentials Verification Organization (CVO): AI verifies healthcare provider credentials in real time across 2,000+ primary sources (state boards, NPDB, licensing/sanctions checks) and drives payer enrolment; claims full credentialing in ~2 business days vs the industry's typical 2-4 months.
