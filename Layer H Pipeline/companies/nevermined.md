## What they do

A decentralized AI-to-AI payment protocol (x402/A2A/AP2-compatible) - agents discover, negotiate and autonomously pay or get paid by other agents or services in real time, with access control, usage metering, credit/billing and settlement, and no per-transaction human click-through.
