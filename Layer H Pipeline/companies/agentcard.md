## What they do

Issues single-use virtual Visa cards for AI agents - a human funds a wallet and sets a budget/scope once, then the agent autonomously executes the full purchase (cart, address, checkout) via native MCP integration with no per-transaction human approval; risk is capped by card balance, not a sign-off gate.
