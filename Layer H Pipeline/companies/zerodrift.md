## What they do

An inline AI firewall that validates every outbound regulated-industry communication (email, CRM, chat) against SEC/FINRA rules and firm policy, then autonomously passes, rewrites or blocks it before it reaches a customer or regulator. 'Escalate' is the exception path for ambiguous cases only, not a default review gate.
