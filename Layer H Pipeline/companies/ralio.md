## What they do

London trust-and-policy checkpoint between business AI agents and payment rails (FPS/BACS/SEPA bank transfers, cards, stablecoins): verifies agent identity, enforces signed mandates and spend limits, logs everything, and executes autonomously above a confidence threshold.
