## Open questions

Veritus's own public messaging is internally inconsistent on exactly the thesis-fit question that matters most: the YC launch post says agents operate 'with perfect consistency, 24/7' with no mention of escalation, while other company materials describe agents that 'escalate appropriately when human judgment is required.' The favourable read - exception-based escalation, not human sign-off, which would distinguish this from the Avallon AI precedent - rests on the more forgiving of two contradictory self-descriptions and should be treated as OPEN, not resolved in the company's favour. First-call: walk through an actual settlement conversation end to end - at what point, if any, does a human review or approve the terms before they reach the borrower? Current recovery/cure rate vs the client's prior human-agent baseline, and the pricing model.
