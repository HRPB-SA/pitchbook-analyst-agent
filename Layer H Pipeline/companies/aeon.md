Durability 7/10 BORDERLINE, Watch HELD - and the 2026-07-30 trigger hit DEGRADES the thesis rather than improving it. AEON announced on 2026-07-29 that it had integrated its settlement layer with bKash and Nagad, Bangladesh's two dominant mobile financial services providers [confirmed that the PR Newswire release exists, carried by StreetInsider and ADVFN]. Three problems. (1) Assess as ANNOUNCEMENT-STAGE, not confirmed live processing: neither counterparty is quoted, neither has issued a statement, no Bangladeshi press pickup. (2) The corridor sits in a jurisdiction with an ENFORCED CRYPTO PROHIBITION, and the named partners are themselves the entities policing it, having blocked thousands of accounts for suspected crypto activity. (3) Most important for us, this is LEGACY HUMAN QR VOLUME, NOT AGENT-NATIVE VOLUME - the release's own consumer mechanic is 'local consumers seamlessly check out at participating merchants using their preferred crypto assets', every named wallet connector is a human-operated consumer wallet, and the agentic language is forward-looking ('allows autonomous AI agents to tap into'). Same pattern as AEON's Bolivia release of 2026-07-09. The standing 07-19 concern is not resolved, it is REINFORCED, and AEON now looks like a serial announcer of national QR-rail integrations under agentic headlines. Hold Watch, do not promote, mark traction quality and governance DOWN.
