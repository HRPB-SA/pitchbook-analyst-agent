### Decision reasoning

PASS 2026-07-30, and it is the consistent call. The AI does the searching, extraction and report assembly end to end, and it is priced per delivered artifact rather than per seat - but IT DOES NOT OWN THE REGULATED DECISION. Its own words are 'generating audit-ready title data in minutes, not days' [confirmed, titl.co 2026-07-31], which is document generation, not underwriting judgment. No public source shows Titl holding a title agent or underwriter licence in any state, or naming an underwriter partner. That is materially the same posture that killed Zaito ('auditable for underwriters') earlier in the same run, so passing Titl keeps the standard consistent. All five sub-scores are 1. SCORING CHANGE, made deliberately and flagged: the underwriter scored Outcome Pricing 2 citing $299 per report, $200 on subscription and ClearTitl at $10 as [confirmed] from titl.co product pages; the validator fetched the live site and found NO PRICING ANYWHERE ON IT - the $299 and $200 figures trace to Refresh Miami, a local outlet, and ClearTitl at $10 could not be corroborated in any source. Downgraded to 1, taking durability 6/10 to 5/10, following the Kay.ai precedent from 07-28 exactly. The Pass verdict is unaffected.
