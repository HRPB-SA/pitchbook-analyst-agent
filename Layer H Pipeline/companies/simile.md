## What they do

AI-driven human-behaviour simulation: 'synthetic users'. Generative agents anchored in a nationally representative panel run enterprise simulation workflows to segment sentiment, test concepts, validate design choices and forecast how people will respond to a change - used for marketing and product research, and pitched as reaching niche populations and de-risking decisions before they ship. Legal name Simile AI, Inc.; founded 2025; HQ 435 Tasso Street, Suite 315, Palo Alto CA 94301. Stated use of the Series B: train the core human-behaviour foundation models, expand simulation compute layers, and scale commercial engineering across enterprise healthcare, financial services and media.

### Decision reasoning

PASS on a CONFIRMED STAGE BREACH, and it is not a close call. A $2.0B post-money is roughly 25x the fund's ~$80M cap - the largest breach recorded in this database, an order of magnitude beyond Kite AI ($156.9M post), which was passed on exactly this basis. Total raised is $300M in about twelve months against a $100K-$500K cheque mandate. There is no entry at any price and no SPV or secondary structure that reaches a company at this valuation from this fund size. SECOND, INDEPENDENT REASON, which would matter even if the price did not: THIS IS OFF-THESIS ON THE AUTONOMY TEST. Simile simulates human responses so that a HUMAN can make a better decision - the output is an insight consumed by a marketer, product team or policy owner. It does not run a job function end to end, it is not the software layer beneath an autonomous machine, and it is not a payment or trust rail for transacting agents. It is decision SUPPORT, which is the category the fund's 'priced on the decision, not the seat' framing is meant to exclude. Logged as a comp; no outreach.
