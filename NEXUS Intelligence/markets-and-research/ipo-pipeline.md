# IPO Pipeline

4 companies tracked for IPO readiness: Anthropic, CoreWeave, Databricks, SpaceX.

## Anthropic

- **Filing status:** Pre-Filing
- **Readiness scores (0-100):** Financial 8 · Governance 6 · Competitive 9
- **Critical blockers:** Gross-vs-net ARR reconciliation in S-1 (contrarian thesis). Three hyperscaler investors who are simultaneously infrastructure providers and board-level stakeholders create governance complexity.
- **Absorption impact:** First mega-cap AI IPO sets the comp framework for all subsequent AI company IPOs. If prices at >$400B, reprices Databricks upward. If prices at discount, creates overhang.
- **Last updated:** 2026-05-12

## Databricks

- **Filing status:** Pre-Filing
- **Readiness scores (0-100):** Financial 9 · Governance 9 · Competitive 9
- **Critical blockers:** OpenAI native integration risk to platform position. Timing depends on market window.
- **Absorption impact:** Cleanest AI IPO candidate. If prices well, opens the window for Anthropic. If Anthropic goes first, Databricks benefits from comp validation.
- **Last updated:** 2026-05-12

## SpaceX
