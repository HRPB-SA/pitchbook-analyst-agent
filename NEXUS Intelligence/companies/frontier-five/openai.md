## (a) Overview

OpenAI was founded December 11, 2015, as a nonprofit AI-safety research lab with a pledged $1 billion commitment from Elon Musk, Sam Altman, Peter Thiel, Reid Hoffman, Jessica Livingston, and Y Combinator. Its corporate form has changed three times since: a March 2019 shift to "capped-profit" OpenAI LP (100x investor return cap); an October 18, 2024 announcement of intent to convert to a for-profit public benefit corporation; and a completed recapitalization into **OpenAI Group PBC** on October 28, 2025, in which the OpenAI Foundation (nonprofit parent) retained approximately 26%, Microsoft approximately 27% (~$135B implied value), and employees/other investors the remaining ~47% — removing the profit cap and giving Sam Altman equity for the first time (source: `openai-ipo-2027.md`, CNBC/Reuters/Al Jazeera/Forbes). Note: an earlier, October 2024-vintage figure in the NEXUS company registry (`legacy_companies_row`) described the nonprofit's post-conversion stake as "~2%" — a much smaller figure than the ~26% that the completed 2025 recapitalization ultimately produced; both figures are preserved here as dated data points rather than reconciled (see Section (i)).

**Leadership and board:** Sam Altman (CEO); Greg Brockman (President/co-founder); Sarah Friar (CFO); Denise Dresser (Chief Revenue Officer, hired September 2025 from CEO of Slack within Salesforce). Board: Bret Taylor (Chair), Sam Altman, Larry Summers, Adam D'Angelo, Sue Desmond-Hellmann, and Adebayo Ogunlesi (added per the most recent NEXUS refresh, dated 2026-08-10). Ilya Sutskever (co-founder/Chief Scientist) departed August 1, 2024, to found Safe Superintelligence (SSI).

**Products:** ChatGPT (consumer, Enterprise, and Government tiers) and the developer API remain the core; OpenAI Codex (browser-based coding agent, launched April 15, 2026, competing with Claude Code) and GPT-5.4/5.5 (agentic/enterprise-positioned models, April 2026) are the current growth push. A self-serve advertising platform launched May 5, 2026, alongside ChatGPT Work (announced later, powered by GPT-5.6). Sora (text-to-video, launched February 2024) was shut down in April 2026 amid a broader reorganization (~$1M/day compute cost cited); OpenAI for Science was disbanded and Prism folded into Codex the same month. A hardware push (AI-native phone, targeting 2028 mass production; earbuds targeted H2 2026) is in early development per one industry-newsletter source (`shared_report_data_points`/`shared_media_outreach`, The Rundown AI, 2026-05-06 — single-source, unverified).

**Scale (most recent dated figures):** ~800–900M ChatGPT weekly active users (WAU) with roughly 85% on the free tier; WAU growth plateaued around 700M in August 2025 against an internal 1B-by-end-2025 goal that was never publicly announced as met (WSJ, 2026-04-27). Headcount: 4,500 as of March 21, 2026 (PitchBook) — down from a 4,000 figure as of February 11, 2026, but confirmed **below** Anthropic's 5,000 (as of April 21, 2026) for the first time on record; a March 2026 internal email (The Information) put an end-2026 target of ~8,000. Two-year employee retention was cited at 67%, against Anthropic's 80% (CNBC, 2026-04-17, "The Big Reset" leadership exodus — see Section (h)).

**Microsoft relationship:** the single most important commercial relationship in OpenAI's structure. Began with a $1B investment in July 2019 (Azure exclusivity) and a further $10B multi-year commitment announced January 10, 2023 (49% of profits until cap, Azure as exclusive cloud). Restructured April 27, 2026: exclusivity ended, the AGI clause was removed, and Microsoft's revenue share was capped at $38B through 2030 (against an uncapped run that would have reached ~$135B — the cap is described internally as saving OpenAI "~$97B" and shifting projected profitability from 2030 to 2029). OpenAI can now license models across any cloud, and announced availability on Amazon Bedrock in 2026 as its first material non-Azure distribution channel. Microsoft's equity stake is reported at 26.79%–27% (see Section (e)).

**Competitive position:** ChatGPT's market/traffic share fell from 86.7% to 64.5% between two 2026 readings (Similarweb/Jefferies data, cited April 2026); by Counterpoint Research's Q1 2026 measure, OpenAI held 29% global LLM revenue share, behind Anthropic's 31.4% — the first quarter Anthropic is reported to have passed OpenAI on that specific metric (Counterpoint Research/Economic Times, 2026-05-01). Enterprise revenue mix is reported at approximately 40% (Counterpoint, cited twice) against Anthropic's ~80%, though a separate NEXUS field (`legacy_companies_row`) shows OpenAI enterprise mix at 15% (down from a prior 25%) — a sizeable, unreconciled gap flagged in Section (i).

**IPO:** OpenAI is in the SEC confidential-filing pipeline, but the filing date, and the expected listing window, are each subject to open, well-documented internal disagreement — see Sections (d) and (i). Sam Altman has reportedly called any IPO valuation below $1 trillion a "nonstarter" (per the dedicated analyst call `openai-ipo-2027.md`, sourced to Bloomberg/NYT, 2026-06-26), against a last confirmed private mark of $852B (March 31, 2026).

### Margins, burn, and the free-tier subsidy finding

- **Gross margin: 33%** — a 13-point miss against a 46% internal target (Sacra, confirmed May 2026; CANONICAL per NEXUS, verified last on 2026-02-27 and still current). An earlier/lower 30% figure appears in a March 26, 2026 financial-model sync; treated as the same metric at an earlier, less-precise reading rather than a substantive conflict.
- **The free-tier subsidy finding (flagged as significant):** OpenAI's FY2025 inference COGS reached **$8.4 billion**. Paying users accounted for approximately 66% of that spend; the remaining ~34% — an estimated **$2.86 billion** — subsidized **910 million non-paying users who generated zero direct revenue**. This cost does not appear in any public disclosure, is not captured in ARR, and (per the source report) is not reflected in consensus valuation frameworks. OpenAI's 2026 launch of a self-serve ad-supported tier is characterized in the source material as "a structural response to this subsidy burden, not a growth initiative." (Source: "The $100 Billion Entry Fee" editorial draft, data as of 2026-03-27.)
- **Net losses:** H1 2025 net loss of **$13.5B** (The Information; flagged "Legacy" vintage by NEXUS). FY2025 GAAP loss reported at roughly **$38.5B**, and Q1 2026 GAAP loss at roughly **$8.5B** — both described as inflated by non-cash charges tied to the PBC conversion and equity remeasurement, with underlying operating burn characterized as "a low-teens-billions annual run-rate" (source: `openai-ipo-2027.md`, which explicitly declines to collapse the GAAP-loss and operating-loss framings into one number). Cumulative losses of **$21B** (current NEXUS figure) vs. **$20B** (prior refresh) are also on record.

Presented plainly, both values shown, none silently resolved.

1. **IPO timing — four distinct estimates in circulation.** (a) NEXUS's own PitchBook-sourced canonical field: **"September 2026"** (window "Sep–Nov 2026"), reaffirmed as recently as a July 9, 2026 financing-note refresh — still current per the conflict register, "staleness objection is dead." (b) The dedicated analyst call `openai-ipo-2027.md` (2026-07-07): base case **H1 2027** (40% probability), with only ~20% probability of a 2026 listing and 15% probability of 2028+, citing a Bloomberg/NYT report (2026-06-26) that CFO Sarah Friar has "reportedly advocated 2027 internally." Kalshi prediction-market pricing (cited in the same note) implies ~59% odds of an IPO *announcement* by March 1, 2027 and ~73% by June 2027 — consistent with a 2027 listing, not a 2026 one. (c) NEXUS's `aibq_unicorn_scores` table and the `profiles/openai.md` snapshot both separately show **"2028+."** (d) `legacy_companies_row` (refreshed 2026-05-04) states plainly, **"IPO planned for 2026."** NEXUS's own conflict register carries this as a formally "Frozen"/"Shifted" conflict and assigns a house probability of **P=0.55** that a public 2027 reaffirmation surfaces before September 30, 2026.
