 Gross margin compression occurs when revenue per unit falls faster than cost per unit, shrinking the percentage of revenue retained after direct costs. In AI inference, the relevant unit is the API call or token — as labs cut per-token prices to compete on cost-efficiency, gross margin compresses unless delivery cost (compute, energy, amortized model training) falls proportionally or faster.<br><br>\*\*Mechanism:\*\* The pricing-to-margin transmission runs in two steps.
