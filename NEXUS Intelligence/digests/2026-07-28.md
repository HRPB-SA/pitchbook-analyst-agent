Pulling from standing gap backlog:<br><br>\*\*Concept: Recurring Revenue Mix as a Quality Signal (ARR% of Total Revenue)\*\*<br><br>\*\*Definition:\*\* Recurring revenue mix is the percentage of a company's total revenue derived from contractually committed, repeating sources — subscriptions, maintenance contracts, SaaS licenses — as opposed to one-time professional services, perpetual license sales, or transactional fees.<br><br>\*\*Mechanism (the why):\*\* A high recurring revenue mix signals three compounding properties: (1) revenue predictability — investors and operato
rs can model forward revenue with higher confidence; (2) customer stickiness — customers who are paying recurring fees have made an ongoing commitment, implying higher switching costs than a one-time buyer; (3) sales efficiency — retaining existing ARR costs less than acquiring new ARR, so a high mix amplifies the leverage of incremental growth. Below \~70%, a business is still heavily transactional; above \~80%, the economics structurally resemble a SaaS business regardless of what the company calls its product.<br><br>\*\*Live anchor:\*\* IFS reporting 84% recurring revenue in H1 2026 \[T2, PR Newswire, 2026-07-28\] is not just a growth-quality indicator — it is a valuation input.
