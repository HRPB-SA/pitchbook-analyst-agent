# §23 FINANCIAL CONCEPT OF THE DAY
**Pre-commencement lease obligations (ASC 842).** Definition: a signed lease creates no balance-sheet entry until the lessee takes possession — at commencement it books a right-of-use asset and matching lease liability at the present value of payments. Mechanism: the standard keys recognition to control of the asset, so a 20-year, \~\$19B lease signed today with H2 2027 service shows up only in the contractual-obligations footnote of an S-1, not in liabilities. Live anchor: this is precisely the Basis Trade mechanism — Anthropic's committed spend is footnote-shaped and time-phased while OpenAI's purchase obligations stack toward \$1.15T; the analyst tool is to rebuild both companies' obligation curves by commencement date, not signature date.
