Mechanism: a rising/falling mark changes cost of capital, counterparty behavior, and comparables, which changes the underlying value, which changes the mark. ### §24 — CFA STUDY (Level I, Dec 2026)
Domain: Corporate Finance. Concept: WACC sensitivity to the risk-free rate.
Vignette: A firm has 60% equity (cost 12%), 40% debt (after-tax cost 5%). If r-f falls 50bp and equity beta is 1.4, the approximate change in WACC is closest to: A) −42bp  B) −30bp  C) −18bp  D) +12bp.
Think in components: equity weight 0.6 × (β 1.4 × −50bp ≈ −70bp) ≈ −42bp on the equity leg; debt leg \~unchanged short-term. **Answer: A (−42bp).** Wrong-answer reasoning: B forgets the 0.6 equity weight; C double-discounts; D wrong sign. Honesty ledger: conceptual (WACC weighting), not careless — the trap is forgetting to scale the beta-driven move by the equity weight.
