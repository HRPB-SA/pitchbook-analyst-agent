nds, compressing the multiple a rational investor is willing to pay — which is why high-multiple software names were the first to sell off in 2022.<br><br>\*Live anchor:\* Databricks at \$188B valuation and \$5.4B ARR \[T2 · Bloomberg, 2026-07-17; T1 canonical\] implies a \~34.8× ARR multiple (est.).
