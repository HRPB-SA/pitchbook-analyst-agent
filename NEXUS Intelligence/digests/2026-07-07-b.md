# §23 FINANCIAL CONCEPT OF THE DAY
**Index-inclusion forced flow (float-adjusted reconstitution).** Definition: when a stock enters a capitalization-weighted index, every fund tracking that index must buy shares to match the new weight, regardless of price. Mechanism: index-tracking demand is price-inelastic — it buys the weight, not the value — so when the required purchase is large relative to the freely-tradeable float, the flow moves price mechanically (the "index inclusion premium"), and the effect scales inversely with float. Live anchor: today's SPCX add — \~\$27B of potential forced demand into a 3–5% float is the extreme case; the analyst tool is to size flow-vs-float (forced \$ ÷ free-float \$) to estimate the mechanical push and separate it from any fundamental re-rating — the same math applies to Anthropic, OpenAI, and Databricks at their listings and future index adds.
