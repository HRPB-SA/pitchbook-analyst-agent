The mechanism: a company can grow revenue aggressively but if the capital required to generate that growth earns less than what investors require, the growth is value-destructive. This is why CFOs care — they are the stewards of capital allocation, not just revenue.<br><br>\*\*Live anchor — the Cursor CFO Council through the ROIC lens:\*\* Cursor's CFO Council is explicitly building a framework for "measuring and maximizing AI ROI" \[T2, \*Fortune\* 2026-07-23\].
