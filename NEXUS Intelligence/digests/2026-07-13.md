s per-unit revenue falls. Denominating in local currency at a locally calibrated price point removes that friction without necessarily cutting the unit economics in USD terms, because local operating costs are also lower.<br><br>\*Live anchor — Anthropic India:\* Anthropic's INR-denominated Claude plans \[T2, TechCrunch, Jul 13\] are a direct application of this mechanism.
