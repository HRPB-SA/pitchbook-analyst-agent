Why this happens

The priciest tokens can do the cheapest work.

Buyers pay for finished tasks, not tokens. A cheaper model that fails more often has to be re-run, and every re-run burns operator time. Count that, and the sticker price stops mattering, the success rate takes over, and the ranking flips.

Cost per completed task = (cost per try + failure cost × chance of failure) ÷ success rate. At the report’s $17 assumption, Claude Opus 4.8 finishes for about $2.56 while Muse Spark, the cheapest sticker, costs about $5.83.
