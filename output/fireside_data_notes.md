## Segment 1: Durability

- VCEP: two XGBoost models + two SHAP models. Out-of-sample accuracy 71%
  (Success vs No-Exit), 94% (M&A vs IPO given a successful exit). Eligibility:
  >=2 VC rounds in trailing 6 years, currently VC-backed, global ex-Greater
  China. Refreshes 6x daily. Opportunity Score = percentile IRR ranking; it is
  the score the IA40 uses. [V]
- IA40: Madrona x PitchBook, 2025 is the 5th annual list. 340+ nominations from
  70+ investors at 54 firms. Uses PitchBook VCEP Opportunity Score as a scoring
  input. 2025: 27 first-time winners, 13 returning (~50% annual turnover). Only
  Databricks has made all five lists; through 2021-24 only four names (Abnormal,
  Databricks, Hugging Face, Runway) made all four. 2025 winners raised $119B
  cumulatively, $86B (>70%) in the trailing 12 months. [V / R]
- Q1 2025 AI/ML VC: record $73.6B across 1,603 deals. Horizontal platforms
  ~$50B across 425 deals (~70% of capital). Vertical apps: 1,022 deals (~60% of
  deals) but only $19.2B. Vertical models cost ~$3M-$10M to build vs hundreds of
  millions for horizontal LLMs. [V]
- SaaS early-stage expected successful-exit rate 72.1%, +5.7 ppt above the #2
  vertical, top-ranked 8+ consecutive years. AI early-stage TTM deal value
  +41.2% (vs +17.5% cross-vertical avg); AI = 37.2% of published patents. [V]
- "SaaS Is Dead, Long Live SaS" (PitchBook, Feb 10 2026): outcome pricing shift
  from ~$1,200/seat/yr to ~$10,000/automated-workflow/yr (~8x unit uplift); the
  60% gross-margin line is the AI-era durability threshold; avoid UI wrappers,
  fund vertical agents with proprietary data moats. [V]
